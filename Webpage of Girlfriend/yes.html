Letter of Apology

Dear Fey,

I am writing this because I am so deeply sorry for the times I've disappointed you and caused you pain. I know I have hurt you, and I am asking from the bottom of my heart for your forgiveness and just one more chance to make things right.

I want you to know something important: you do not need to change a single thing about yourself. Please don't ever lose your clingy side, your fierce side, or whatever else makes you, YOU. Stay exactly as you are, because that's the person I fell in love with. I am the one who needs to grow, change, and be better for us.

Please don't change, and please don't leave me. Keep loving me exactly the way you do right now. I love you so much, more than words can say.

Yours,
Ken
